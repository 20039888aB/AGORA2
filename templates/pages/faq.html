Getting Started

How do I sign up for AgoraProf?

Getting started with AgoraProf is simple:

  • Visit our signup page and choose your plan
  • Fill in your organization details
  • Complete the payment process (if applicable)
  • Access your dashboard and start creating courses

We also offer a 14-day free trial for Professional and Enterprise plans.

What do I need to get started?

To get started with AgoraProf, you'll need:

  • A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  • Stable internet connection
  • Your organization's basic information
  • Course content you want to upload (optional)

That's it! No software installation required - everything runs in the cloud.

Can I import existing courses?

Yes! AgoraProf supports importing courses from various formats:

  • SCORM compliant packages
  • Video files (MP4, WebM)
  • PDF documents
  • PowerPoint presentations
  • Common image formats

Our migration team can also help with large-scale course transfers.

Features & Functionality

What types of content can I create?

AgoraProf supports a wide variety of content types:

  • Video lessons and lectures
  • Interactive quizzes and assessments
  • Assignments and projects
  • Discussion forums and chat
  • Live streaming sessions
  • Downloadable resources
  • SCORM packages

All content can be organized into modules and learning paths.

Does AgoraProf support mobile learning?

Absolutely! AgoraProf is fully mobile-responsive and offers:

  • Native iOS and Android apps
  • Progressive Web App (PWA) support
  • Offline mode for downloaded content
  • Push notifications for course updates
  • Mobile-optimized interface

Learners can access courses anytime, anywhere, on any device.

Can I customize the platform?

Yes! Customization options vary by plan:

  • All plans: Basic branding options
  • Professional: Advanced customization
  • Enterprise: Full white-label solution
  • Custom: Complete platform customization

Customization includes logos, colors, domains, and even custom features.

Pricing & Billing

How does pricing work?

Our pricing is based on two main factors:

  • Number of users: More users = higher tier plan
  • Features needed: Advanced features require higher tiers

All plans include core features like course creation, user management, and basic analytics. Higher tiers add advanced features like API access, custom branding, and priority support.

Can I change my plan anytime?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time:

  • Upgrades: Take effect immediately with prorated billing
  • Downgrades: Take effect at next billing cycle
  • Cancellations: No long-term commitments on monthly plans

We'll always notify you before any billing changes.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes! We offer several discount options:

  • Annual billing: Save 20% compared to monthly
  • Non-profits: Special pricing for educational institutions
  • Volume discounts: For large organizations
  • Custom quotes: For enterprise needs

Contact our sales team to learn more about available discounts.

Technical Support

What kind of support do you offer?

Support levels vary by plan:

  • Starter: Email support during business hours
  • Professional: Priority email and chat support
  • Enterprise: 24/7 phone, email, and chat support
  • Custom: Dedicated account manager and SLA

All plans have access to our comprehensive knowledge base and documentation.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely! Security is our top priority:

  • 256-bit SSL encryption for all data
  • SOC 2 Type II certified data centers
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • GDPR and CCPA compliant
  • Automatic daily backups with point-in-time recovery

We never share your data with third parties without your explicit consent.

What about integrations?

AgoraProf integrates with popular business tools:

  • HR systems (Workday, BambooHR)
  • CRM platforms (Salesforce, HubSpot)
  • Communication tools (Slack, Microsoft Teams)
  • Video conferencing (Zoom, Teams)
  • Productivity suites (Google Workspace, Office 365)

Enterprise plans also include custom API integrations.
