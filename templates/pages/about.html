About AgoraProf

Empowering organizations worldwide with intelligent learning management solutions that drive growth, innovation, and excellence.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, AgoraProf emerged from a simple observation: traditional learning management systems were failing to meet the evolving needs of modern organizations. We saw a gap between what businesses needed and what existing platforms provided.

Our journey began with a small team of passionate educators, technologists, and business leaders who shared a common vision: to create a learning platform that truly understands the unique challenges of today's workplace.

Today, we're proud to serve over 500 organizations worldwide, helping them deliver exceptional learning experiences that drive real business results.
